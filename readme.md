# UNSEEN ECOSYSTEM

An ecosystem of interconnected tools, systems, and experiences designed to adapt to your digital lifestyle.

The **Unseen Ecosystem** brings together projects built around flexibility, customization, and unified design.

### UNSEEN APP

A multi-tool productivity and automation platform designed to unify essential digital tools in one place — including **Email, LLM Chat, Notes, Calendar, and coding tools**.

### UNSEEN TREE

A highly customizable alternative to traditional link-in-bio platforms, featuring **eight predefined design systems**, each available in both **dark and light themes**.

---

## Built to Adapt

We believe digital tools shouldn't force you into a predefined workflow.

The **Unseen Ecosystem** is designed to be flexible, customizable, and adaptable to the way you work and live.

Feel free to **use, modify, explore, and fork** these projects.

**Building systems that adapt to your lifestyle not the other way around.**
